AayuHub Medical Knowledge Database
Project Documentation
This document describes the purpose, information structure, and operational rules of the AayuHub medical knowledge database. It is a human-readable reference document; database commands and source code have been intentionally omitted.
1. Overview
The AayuHub Medical Knowledge Database is designed to support a healthcare information chatbot. It organizes medical departments, example conditions, associated symptoms, department-specific follow-up questions, emergency warning signs, department-routing guidance, and chatbot safety rules. The information can help the chatbot understand a user's reported concern and guide them toward an appropriate speciality or urgent care.
The database is organized around four specialities: Dermatology, Cardiology, Orthopaedics, and Ophthalmology.

6. Emergency Warning Signs
The source database lists warning signs that should prompt urgent evaluation rather than routine department guidance. These signs are presented here as recorded in the source.
Dermatology
Severe allergic reaction
Breathing difficulty
Rapidly spreading infection with fever
Severe blistering or skin peeling
Cardiology
Chest pressure or pain
Severe breathlessness
Fainting
Sweating with chest discomfort
Pain spreading to the arm or jaw
Orthopaedics
Suspected fracture
Visible deformity
Severe accident injuries
New limb weakness
Back pain with loss of bladder or bowel control
Ophthalmology
Sudden vision loss
Severe eye pain
Eye injury
Chemical exposure
Sudden flashes or many new floaters
Safety note: If any emergency warning sign is present, the user should seek immediate medical care or contact local emergency services. This document is not a diagnostic tool.
7. Department Routing Guidance
Routing is intended to help direct a user to a relevant speciality. It should not be treated as confirmation of a disease or a substitute for clinical assessment.
8. Chatbot Behaviour and Safety Rules
Identify symptoms before suggesting a department.
Ask follow-up questions if the information provided is insufficient.
Never claim to provide a confirmed medical diagnosis.
Never prescribe medicines or dosages.
Check for emergency symptoms before recommending a hospital.
Direct emergency cases to immediate medical care.
Recommend hospitals only when the required speciality is listed as available in the hospital database.
9. Information Relationships
The database's information is linked conceptually in the following way:
Each department has a name, code, and description.
Medical conditions are associated with a department.
Each condition can have multiple symptom labels.
Each department can have multiple follow-up questions.
Each department can have multiple emergency warning signs.
Routing guidance connects symptom groups to a department.
Chatbot rules define the safety and interaction behaviour expected of the assistant.
10. Scope and Limitations
The documented content covers four specialities only: Dermatology, Cardiology, Orthopaedics, and Ophthalmology.
Condition descriptions and symptom lists are brief reference labels, not comprehensive clinical criteria.
Symptoms can overlap across conditions, and a symptom match cannot establish a diagnosis.
The source material mentions hospital recommendations only when the required speciality is present in a hospital database; hospital records themselves are not detailed in this document.
The source does not provide treatment protocols, medication guidance, or dosage recommendations, and the chatbot rules explicitly prohibit prescribing medicines or dosages.
11. Summary
AayuHub's medical knowledge data is structured to help a chatbot collect symptom information, ask relevant follow-up questions, identify emergency warning signs, and route users toward one of four medical specialities. Its central safeguards are to avoid confirmed diagnoses, avoid prescribing medication, and prioritize immediate care when emergency symptoms are reported.
